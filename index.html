GAMEPLAY

WHAT'S INSIDE

ONE-THUMB ARCADE

Drag to steer. The floors keep rising and speeding up; fall through the gaps before the spikes catch you.

3 LIVES PER RUN

Red hazards and spikes cost a life. Run out and you can continue once with a token you earned in the game.

YOUR STYLE

Unlock ball skins, trails, worlds, effects and UI themes with coins you collect while playing.

DAILY & SEASONS

Three daily challenges, a 7-day login calendar, achievements and seasonal events with their own pass.

COMPETE

Global, weekly and friends leaderboards, weekly tournaments and score challenges you share with a code.

FAIR PLAY

Scores are checked on the server. No pay-to-win: real-money items are cosmetic only.
