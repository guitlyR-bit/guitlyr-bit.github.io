Playing without an account

You can play without registering. Your progress (coins, items, settings and statistics) is stored on your device.

We do not access your contacts, location or photos, and we never sell your data. The ad provider below may use your device's advertising ID, as described there.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store and verified through RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy). We never see your payment details.

Advertising

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (to continue a run or double a run's coins) and, at most once every few games, a full-screen ad between games. Never during play. The "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the full-screen ads.

Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show, measure and (with your consent) personalise ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent with Google's consent dialog before any ad loads; you can change your choice any time under Settings > Ad privacy. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Processors

Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in the European Union), RevenueCat (purchase verification) and Google AdMob (ads).

Legal basis (GDPR)

Performing the service you request (online features, purchases), legitimate interest (security, anti-cheat, error reports) and consent (usage data, optional email, notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

Your rights and deletion

In the game: Profile → Delete online account deletes your online account and everything linked to it: profile, scores, friends, challenges, tournament results, backup, feedback, usage events and error reports. You can also contact us to access, correct or delete your data, and you may complain to your data-protection authority.

Retention

Online data is kept until you delete your account. Inactive accounts: deleted after 24 months without sign-in. Usage events and error reports: kept for up to 13 months.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
